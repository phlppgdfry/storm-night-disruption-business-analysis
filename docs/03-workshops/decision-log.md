# Decision log

> **What this is:** every decision that shaped the solution, who owned it, and what we gave up. **For:** anyone asking "why is it like this?" six months from now. **Previous:** [policy workshop](policy-workshop.md) · **Next:** [AS-IS process](../04-process/as-is.md).

*Fictional decisions and people.*

| ID | Decision | Owner | Options considered | Why | What we gave up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **Measure time to certainty, not rebooking speed** | S-01 Operations | Calls handled per hour · average delay · time to certainty | The reconstruction showed agents were not the bottleneck and the average delay is set by capacity. What hurts customers is not knowing. | A KPI customer service already reports |
| D-02 | **The system proposes, the duty manager decides** | S-01 Operations | Fully automatic send · approval per message by customer service · one plan approval by the duty manager | 900 approvals is not a control; zero approvals is not trusted (Q-17). One review of the plan with overrides keeps a person accountable. | ±30 minutes of certainty per storm (A-30) |
| D-03 | **Plugs, ADR slots and deck height become sailing attributes in the booking platform**, maintained by load planning | S-07 PO | Keep the spreadsheet and import it · call the load planner per booking · attributes in the platform | One source, readable at 2 a.m., testable. Load planning keeps ownership of the values. | A small change to load planning's routine; an integration task |
| D-04 | **Allocation policy: the recommended policy** (R-15, R-07, R-08, R-09, R-10) | S-01 Operations; S-02, S-03, S-05 consulted | Four policies, simulated on the same 200 nights | See [policy workshop](policy-workshop.md) | Contract customers do not get absolute priority; non-contract customers wait ±0.6 h longer than under pure booking order |
| D-05 | **Commodity code and deliver-by time mandatory for reefer bookings** | S-02 Sales (commercial impact), S-07 PO | Keep free text · text mining on remarks · mandatory fields | The policy can only protect perishables it can see (Q-16, A-05). Text mining was tested on 200 remarks by hand: too ambiguous ("fresh", "chilled", "FR"). | Some friction for customers in the portal; EDI customers get a warning first, refusal after 3 months |
| D-06 | **Add R-15 scarce slots first** after the first simulation run | S-05 Load planner | Reserve ADR lane metres per sailing · place ADR first · accept the drift | Simplest rule that removed the drift in all 200 nights; no unit loses more than one sailing because of it | ADR units can overtake earlier-booked trailers |
| D-07 | **The business case counts goodwill paid, not claims rejected** | S-12 Finance | Count cargo value at risk · count claims filed · count goodwill actually paid | Weather is force majeure (Q-14): counting cargo value would have tripled the benefit and lost Finance in the first meeting | A smaller headline number |
| D-08 | **Pilot before winter, with a storm drill as business acceptance** | S-01 Operations | Wait for a real storm to test · big-bang · drill on a replayed storm | A real storm is the worst moment to find a defect; a drill can be repeated | Two half-days of key-user time |
| D-09 | **Driver texts use the booking contact; numbers kept 30 days** | S-11 Architect (with the DPO) | Collect driver numbers at the gate · use the haulier's office e-mail · booking contact number | Q-18: e-mail is not read at night. The booking contact is already a legitimate contact; 30 days covers claims questions | Some texts reach a dispatcher instead of the driver |
| D-11 | **A smaller ship overrides stability** — when a sailing loses capacity, confirmed units that no longer fit are re-planned first and escalated (R-16) | S-01 Operations | Keep R-11 absolute and let the load planner bump · re-plan silently · re-plan and escalate | Storm drill finding F-5: a vessel swap left 6 reefers confirmed without plugs. Silent re-planning breaks the promise in the message; escalation means customer service calls those customers first | R-11 is no longer absolute; the customer message says "will not move to a later sailing *unless the vessel changes*" |
| D-10 | **Reroute and pull-forward offers move to release 2** | S-07 PO | All in release 1 · release 2 | They need customer consent flows and the Thames route's capacity data; release 1 already reaches the goals in the simulation | Some units sail later than they could |
